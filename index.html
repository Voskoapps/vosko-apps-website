The studio

Small studio, made with care

Vosko Apps is an independent studio from the Netherlands. We design, build and maintain everything ourselves — from game design and 3D art to the code and the store pages.

No big team and no endless meetings: just games and apps that do what they promise, and that keep improving for as long as people use them.

Privacy first

We collect as little as possible and never sell your data. Clear privacy pages for every product.

Fair play

No dark patterns or pay-to-win. If something costs money, we say exactly what you get.

Cozy by design

Warm art, gentle pacing and a real challenge — games you want to return to, not feel pushed into.

Built to last

Every game, app and asset pack is maintained and updated long after launch.

Let's talk

Questions about a game, feedback, press or a business enquiry? We read every email and usually reply within a few days.
